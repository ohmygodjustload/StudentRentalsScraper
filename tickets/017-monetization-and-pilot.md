# Monetization and La Crosse pilot

## Context

Pick a revenue model that stays cheap for independent landlords, and run a pilot in one college town to prove the two-sided market works. Part of epic 011; depends on 012–016.

## Acceptance criteria

- Revenue model chosen and documented. Options to compare: a free basic listing with a paid featured placement, a low flat monthly fee per landlord, a small fee per lease signed, or sponsorships from local businesses. Renters stay free.
- Pilot plan for La Crosse:
  - seed supply by personally recruiting independent landlords (flyers, landlord associations, direct outreach), with their consent;
  - seed demand through campus channels before the fall lease cycle;
  - time the launch around the late-summer to October renewal and signing period.
- Success metrics defined up front, for example the number of active listings, inquiries per listing, leases signed through the platform, and landlord retention after one cycle.
- Go/no-go review after one lease cycle, with a decision to expand to a second market, change direction, or stop.

## Notes

- Do not import listings from the student rentals site or from this scraper. Landlords must create or approve their own listings.
