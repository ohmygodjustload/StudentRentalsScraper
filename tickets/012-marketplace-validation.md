# Market validation and differentiation

## Context

Before building anything, confirm the problem is real and that the product is meaningfully different from existing options. Part of epic 011.

## Acceptance criteria

- Competitor table covering at least Zillow Rental Manager, Apartments.com, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, local college housing boards, and the current student rentals site. For each: cost to landlords, cost to renters, who actually lists there, and weaknesses.
- Verify current landlord pricing on the big sites. If they are free for small landlords, the pitch cannot be "cheaper to list"; record the real differentiator instead.
- Interviews with at least 5 independent landlords and 10 renters in the pilot city. Capture: how they list or search today, what it costs, what is frustrating, and whether they would switch.
- One-paragraph positioning statement and a short list of what the MVP will and will not do.

## Notes

- Candidate differentiators: local focus per city, early "available next lease cycle" listings, curated affordable-but-decent units, simpler tools for owners with 1–10 units.
- Kill criteria: if landlords already list for free and renters are satisfied with current options, stop or pivot before building.
