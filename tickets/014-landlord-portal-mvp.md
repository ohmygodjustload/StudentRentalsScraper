# Landlord portal MVP

## Context

Independent landlords need a fast, low-cost way to list units and respond to renters. Part of epic 011; depends on 013.

## Acceptance criteria

- Landlord sign-up and login with email verification.
- Create, edit, pause, and delete properties and units: address, rent, beds and baths, utilities included, amenities, photos, and lease terms.
- Availability windows: "available now", "available on a date", or "may be available next lease cycle", so landlords can surface units before renewal decisions are made.
- Inbox for renter inquiries with email notifications.
- Simple dashboard: active listings, views, and inquiries per listing.
- Listing form takes under 10 minutes for one unit on a phone.

## Notes

- Address geocoding and enrichment (distance, walk and bike time, walk score) run automatically after save.
- Keep the landlord flow simple; target owners with 1–10 units, not property-management companies.
