# Renter portal MVP

## Context

Renters need to find affordable, decent units and contact landlords directly, including units that will open up next lease cycle. Part of epic 011; depends on 013.

## Acceptance criteria

- Search with filters: rent range, beds and baths, availability date, utilities, pets, and distance or travel time to a market anchor (campus, downtown).
- Map and list views.
- Listing detail page with photos, enrichment data (walk and bike time, walk score), and a contact form.
- Saved searches with email alerts for new matches.
- Optional renter accounts; browsing works without signing in.
- Mobile-friendly layout.

## Notes

- A deal or value score (see ticket 001) could rank results, but it must use only objective listing data. Do not rank or filter by neighborhood demographics (see ticket 016).
